## WARDROBE RULE
Muted palette only: charcoal, olive, navy, rust, cream.
NO pure white - it blows out under window light.
NO purple in any wardrobe. #7C4DFF belongs to the brand, not the clothes.
If a character wears purple, the caption highlight stops reading as Wopie.

## OFFICE ENVIRONMENT  (identical in all 10 scenes)
Small open-plan startup room. Plain light-grey wall behind the group.
Two monitors on desks at the frame edges, screens dim or off.
A whiteboard camera-right, WIPED BLANK - never with writing on it.
One plant, one coffee cup. Nothing else. No posters, no signage.
Reason: any text Higgsfield renders will come out wrong. Keep frames clean
so all real text is composited in CapCut.

## CAMERA TREATMENT
Static tripod, eye level, chest-up-to-waist framing on the group.
No handheld shake. No zoom. No dolly.
The ONLY exception: S2 is a very slow push toward the Team Lead.
Documentary flatness. If a shot looks cinematic, it is wrong.

## GROUP POSITIONS  (identical in every wide shot - this is what sells the lock)
  Team Lead   - centre, front, roughly 1.5m from camera
  Teammate A  - behind and to his left
  Teammate B  - behind and to his right
  Teammate C  - behind centre, half a step further back
Nobody moves position between scenes. Only expressions change.
