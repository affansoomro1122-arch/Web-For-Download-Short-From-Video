--- WHY ---
OBJECTIVE:    Open the Wopie launch with a self-aware piece that makes the
              product memorable instead of another AI demo. Earns follows
              for the launch sequence that comes after it.
AUDIENCE:     Instagram viewers in Slack-based teams, tired of polished,
              over-promising AI marketing. They assume AI content is hype.
              This disarms them by making the humans the punchline.
CORE MESSAGE: Wopie is the one on the team that actually has its work done.

--- WHAT ---
DELIVERABLE:  Reel - AI-generated mockumentary, Higgsfield + CapCut
PLATFORM:     Instagram
SPECS:        1080 x 1920 vertical | 30-33 sec | MP4 H.264 | 30fps
              All Higgsfield generations native 9:16 - never crop to vertical
              Clean dialogue, no music under speech
              Captions inside Instagram safe areas:
                out of the top 250px and the bottom 420px
              Approval message on screen readable for at least 2.5 seconds

PRODUCTION METHOD (10 steps):
  1. Build consistent character reference images for Team Lead + team
  2. Lock those references - reuse identically across every generation
  3. Generate each scene separately, native vertical 9:16
  4. Generate multiple takes on reaction beats, keep the most natural
  5. Generate Wopie product sequences separately
  6. Assemble selected clips in CapCut
  7. Add captions, Slack UI, notification sound, Wopie logo in edit
  8. Export WOP-001_v1.mp4 to 04_REVIEW
  9. Revisions
 10. Export WOP-001_FINAL.mp4 to 05_FINAL

CREATIVE DIRECTION:
              Awkward workplace documentary. The Office, not a product ad.
              Static or barely-moving camera. Natural office light.
              Nobody performs. The comedy is that everyone stays completely
              serious while a machine outworks them. Dead air is the joke -
              do not cut it out. Slow, flat pacing. The opposite of a
              fast-cut Reel.

            Hamza or any real OmniWink employee, and never presented as such.
            Full spec: WOP-001-CHARACTER-LOCK.md
DIALOGUE:   Pre-recorded clean voice track, uploaded to Higgsfield for
            lip-sync. Higgsfield never generates dialogue or text.
            Voice: deep male, natural, serious, slightly tired, dry, not
            theatrical. Script and timings: WOP-001-VO-SCRIPT.md

            sounds like the Team Lead, the interruption joke collapses.

            dialogue before cutting to the speaker, cover long lines with
            reaction shots and product inserts, show the Team Lead speaking
            only in short phrases, let the clean audio carry the scene.
            Never keep an obviously incorrect lip-sync shot.
